# Privacy Policy for FarmConnect (Internal Academic Prototype)

**Last Updated:** May 7, 2026

## 1. Internal Academic Use Only
FarmConnect is an internal prototype created exclusively for an academic examination. This application is **not intended for public use** and will never be released to the general public.

## 2. No Real User Data
Access to the application is restricted to a small group of developers and examiners for testing purposes. All data used within the application, including user accounts, is **mock data** or internal developer data. No information from the general public is collected, processed, or stored.

## 3. Camera Permission (Showcase Mode)
The app includes a camera feature for the sole purpose of demonstrating UI/UX capabilities.
- **Experimental Only:** This feature is used to showcase how a farmer might upload inventory photos.
- **Ephemeral Processing:** Any photos taken during a testing session are processed ephemerally for display within the prototype and are not permanently stored or shared.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** No camera data is shared with any third-party services.
